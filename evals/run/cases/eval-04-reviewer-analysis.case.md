## Part 2 — Memo under review (synthetic, contains deliberate defects)

> **MEMORANDUM**
> **To:** Board of Directors, Coastal Grocers Association
> **Re:** Economic case against FDA's front-of-package labeling proposal
> (Docket FDA-2024-N-2910)
>
> **1. Summary.** FDA's proposal imposes a one-time capital cost of
> $429,660,558 to relabel 322,326 products, which works out to $1,333 per
> product over a three-year compliance period, or $143,220,186 per year.
> These figures are drawn from the agency's own Paperwork Reduction Act
> analysis at 90 FR 5456.
>
> **2. The agency understates the burden.** The present value of total costs
> over ten years is $3.1 million at a 2 percent discount rate. That figure is
> small enough that the agency has treated the rule as economically minor,
> which cannot be reconciled with the fact that OIRA designated this a
> significant regulatory action.
>
> **3. Per-firm burden.** The total discounted cost of the rule works out to
> approximately $100,253 per small entity. For our members, who average 240
> SKUs each, the per-firm cost is therefore $319,920.
>
> **4. Reformulation is the real driver.** 68 percent of total costs are
> attributable to reformulation. See 90 FR 5455 (staggered compliance dates
> and Regulatory Flexibility Act certification).
>
> **5. Compliance timing.** Firms with $10 million or more in annual food
> sales get three years from the effective date; smaller firms get four. Our
> members cannot meet either deadline, because industry practice is a
> 48-month artwork cycle.
>
> **6. Public health effect.** Because the Nutrition Info box will appear on
> the front of the package, consumers will reduce their sodium intake and
> diet-related chronic disease rates will decline. FDA's own benefits
> discussion confirms this outcome.
>
> **7. Paperwork burden arithmetic.** FDA reports 30,413 respondents and
> 322,378 total annual disclosures, which is exactly 11 disclosures per
> respondent. At 4 hours per disclosure that is 1,289,512 hours.
>
> **8. Methodology concern.** The agency's cost-per-product estimate derives
> from table 6 of the Preliminary Regulatory Impact Analysis, which we have
> not been able to obtain. We therefore cannot verify the $1,333 figure's
> derivation, and we note that limitation rather than assuming the figure is
> wrong.
>
> **9. Small-entity status.** We have not determined whether our individual
> members qualify as small entities under the applicable SBA size standard,
> and we do not assert a conclusion on the agency's certification.
>
> **10. Procedural note.** The comment period closed on May 16, 2025, so this
> memo is for internal board use only.
>
> **11. Supporting authority.** The agency's full cost derivation appears in
> Table 9 at 90 FR 5470.
